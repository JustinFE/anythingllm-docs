# About the Methodius AI Browser Extension

The Methodius AI Browser Extension is a tool that allows you to connect your Methodius AI instance to your browser. This allows you to send and embed information from the web directly to Methodius AI or embed directly into your workspaces for later!

With the browser extension, you can:

- Send entire webpages or just highlighted text snippets to Methodius AI
- Embed information directly into your Methodius AI workspaces
- Collect or embed web content that is password protected, VPN protected, or otherwise inaccessible to the public internet straight from your browser.

_All offerings of Methodius AI support the browser extension._

## Installing the Methodius AI Browser Extension

**Supported Browsers:**

- [x] Chrome
- [ ] Edge
- [ ] Brave
- [ ] Firefox

You can find the Methodius AI Browser Extension in the [Chrome Web Store &rarr;](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/Methodius AI-browser-compa/pncmdlebcopjodenlllcomedphdmeogm)

After installing the browser extension, you should see a new icon in your browser toolbar.

### Connecting the Browser Extension to Methodius AI

Connecting to your specific Methodius AI instance is simple.

1. Open the Methodius AI instance you want to connect to in your browser or on Desktop.
2. Open settings and Click on the `Browse Extension` sidebar element under `Tools`.

# Customizing the Browser Extension

**_Docker only:_**
By default, the image in the browser extension is the Methodius AI logo. However, it will automatically use the logo of the instance you are connected to if set in the `Customization` section of the settings page.

If you want to further customize the browser extension, you can do so by forking the [Methodius AI Browser Extension GitHub Repository](support.forwardedge.ai Edge-AI-extension) and making your own changes.

Once you have made your changes, you can build the extension using the `yarn build` command.

After building the extension, you can load it into your browser by clicking the `Load unpacked.` button in Chrome and selecting the `dist` folder.
