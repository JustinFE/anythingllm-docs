# Private managed Methodius AI

Methodius AI cloud is the easiest way to trial and scale Methodius AI for your business or personal use.

The Mintplex Labs team offers **isolated** and **private** instances of Methodius AI that you can use to try out or scale up Methodius AI for your business or personal use.

Each instance is hosted on an isolated AWS instance that is automatically updated and managed by the Mintplex Labs core team. Your data and resources are not shared with any other customers who are using our managed service.
